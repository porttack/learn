🌲 Tricampus Conquest

a Risk-style game on a not-to-scale map

🌲 Declare war in the redwoods

Take turns on one screen. Claim every building to win. Rules are in the box below.

How to play
  1. Reinforce. Each turn you get one army for every 3 buildings (at least 3), plus a bonus for holding a whole region. Click your buildings to place them.
  2. Cards. Capture at least one building in a turn and you earn a card at the end. Three of a kind, or one of each kind, trade in for armies. The first set is worth 4, then 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, and 5 more each time after. If you own a building on a traded card you get +2 armies there. A wild card fits anywhere. Hold 5 cards and you must trade. Wipe out a player and you take their cards.
  3. Attack. Click your building with 2 or more armies, then a touching enemy. You roll up to 3 dice, the defender up to 2; highest dice are compared, ties go to the defender. Tick "keep rolling" to attack until you win or run out. After a capture you choose how many extra armies to send in.
  4. Fortify. Once per turn, slide armies between your own connected buildings (1 per click), then end your turn.
  5. Alliances. Allied countries (a heart on the scoreboard) cannot attack each other and win together.
  6. Players can be Human or Bot. Last country standing wins.

Nothing is saved or sent anywhere. Reload and the game is gone.

## What happens

A thousand years after a war destroyed the world, a poisonous jungle full of giant insects called Ohmu is spreading across the land. Nausicaä is the young princess of the Valley of the Wind, a small village that the jungle's poison has not reached. When a neighboring army invades the Valley and wants to burn the jungle, Nausicaä discovers a secret about it and tries to stop the fighting before it destroys everyone.

## Why we watch it

This is Miyazaki's first big film, and it is about taking care of the world, choosing peace over revenge and trying to understand something before you decide to destroy it. It is rated PG for violence: there are war scenes and a death in the first part, so it suits older students better than the other films on this list. It is slower and more serious than most animated movies. The worksheet is all reflection, with no computer science.

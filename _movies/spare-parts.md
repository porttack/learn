## What happens

Based on a true story. Four students at a high school in Phoenix, most of them undocumented, are talked into entering a college underwater robotics competition by a teacher. They have a tiny budget, no experience and a lot of people who expect them to fail. They are up against the best engineering schools in the country, including MIT.

## Why we watch it

This is the story of the MATE ROV competition, the same kind of competition our robotics class enters. Students see what engineering with a small budget looks like, why a team needs people with different strengths, and why creativity can beat money. Note the PG-13 rating: it includes some themes about immigration and family struggles.

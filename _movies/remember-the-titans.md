## What happens

Based on a true story. In 1971, T.C. Williams High School in Alexandria, Virginia, combines its Black and white students for the first time, and the football team has to combine too. Herman Boone, a Black coach, is named head coach over Bill Yoast, a white coach who had expected the job. At training camp the players are made to room with, eat with and learn about teammates they would never have picked. Back home, the team faces a town that is not sure it wants them to succeed, and the players have to decide what they owe each other.

## Why we watch it

This is a film about how people who start out as strangers, or worse, become a team. Students watch characters make hard choices about speaking up, changing their minds and standing by a friend when it costs them something. It is a strong fit for conversations about respect, leadership and what it takes to build a community.

**Content note:** Rated PG, but the film shows racism, including slurs, threats and a serious car accident. It is a historical story and handles these with care, but it is worth a short heads up before the film starts.

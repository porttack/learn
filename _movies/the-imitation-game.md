## What happens

During World War II, mathematician Alan Turing joins a secret team in England trying to break Enigma, the German machine that scrambles military messages. Turing's odd personality clashes with the team, and his plan is to beat the code with a machine instead of a person. After the war the country he helped save treats him terribly.

## Why we watch it

Turing is called the father of computer science, and his story connects computing to ethics, secrecy and fairness. The film shows how outsiders and different points of view solve problems others cannot. Note the PG-13 rating and the heavy themes of war and discrimination: this is the best fit for older students.

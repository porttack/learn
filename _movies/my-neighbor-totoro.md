## What happens

Two sisters, Satsuki and Mei, move to the countryside with their father to be closer to their mother, who is sick in the hospital. In the woods near their new house they meet gentle forest spirits, including the huge, fuzzy Totoro. The film is quiet and slow, and a lot of it is about how the girls handle worry and fear.

## Why we watch it

This is a calm movie that holds a room without anyone running it. It shows kindness without being asked, curiosity about someone who is different, and a brave kid carrying more than she lets on. The worksheet is about character and feelings, with no computer science.

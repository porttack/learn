## What happens

Hiro is a robotics prodigy in the city of San Fransokyo. After a tragedy, he is left with Baymax, an inflatable healthcare robot his older brother built. Hiro upgrades Baymax and teams up with a group of friends to find out who is behind a masked villain's attacks. Along the way he has to decide what to do with his talent.

## Why we watch it

Hiro and his friends test, break and redesign again and again, which is what real engineers do. Baymax is a good example of a machine that follows its programming and still surprises us. The film also asks what you should do with a skill: use it to hurt someone or to help.

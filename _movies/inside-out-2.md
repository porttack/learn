## What happens

Riley is a teenager now, and new emotions have moved into headquarters: Anxiety, Envy, Ennui and Embarrassment. While Riley tries to make the high school hockey team, Anxiety takes charge and starts rebuilding who Riley thinks she should be. Joy and the original emotions have to find their way back.

## Why we watch it

Middle school students are in the middle of this exact stage. The movie shows that every emotion, including the uncomfortable ones, is a normal part of growing up, and that trying to be perfect is a trap. The worksheet is all reflection, with no computer science.

## What happens

Filmmaker Daniel Roher is about to become a father. Anxious about the world his child will grow up in, he sets out to understand artificial intelligence. He interviews people who worry that it could go very wrong, people who are excited about what it could do, and leaders of AI companies including Sam Altman of OpenAI, Dario Amodei of Anthropic and Demis Hassabis of Google DeepMind. The film covers deepfakes, data centers and the race to build more powerful AI, and it does not claim to have the answer.

## Why we watch it

This is a recent documentary about the technology students use every day. It helps them see that there is a debate, that smart people disagree, and that the choices about AI are made by people. It is also good practice in media literacy: who is being interviewed, who declined to be, and who benefits from each answer. It is rated PG-13 (Common Sense Media says 13 and up), mainly for language and some frightening talk about worst-case outcomes. The worksheet has a computer science focus.

## What happens

Ralph is the villain of an old arcade game called Fix-It Felix Jr. After thirty years of being the bad guy, he leaves his game to win a hero's medal in another one. His trip takes him through a shooting game and a candy racing game, where he meets Vanellope von Schweetz, a kid who is treated as a glitch and is not allowed to race. His mistakes put every game in the arcade at risk.

## Why we watch it

Ralph is told who he is by the job his game gives him, and the film is about whether you have to be what other people say you are. It is funny and fast, which helps the room, and it has a light computer science connection (game characters follow their code, a glitch is a bug). One question on the worksheet uses that. It is rated PG and has some cartoon action and rude humor.

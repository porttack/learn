## What happens

Mei Lee is a thirteen-year-old in Toronto in 2002 who wants to be a good daughter and also to have a life of her own. When her feelings get big, she turns into a giant red panda. Her mother says there is a ritual that can remove the panda, but Mei starts to wonder if she wants it gone, especially with a boy band concert coming up that her friends want to go to.

## Why we watch it

Turning Red is about growing up, family expectations and learning to accept the parts of yourself that feel embarrassing or messy. It is the most directly about being a middle schooler of the films on this list. Some content may need a heads up: the panda is a comic stand-in for puberty, and there are jokes about periods and crushes. The worksheet is all reflection, with no computer science.

## What happens

A shipwreck leaves a robot named Roz alone on a wild island. Her programming says to complete tasks, but nobody on the island has any tasks for her. To survive she has to learn the animals' language, earn their trust, and, in an unexpected way, become part of a family. Then a harsh winter threatens everyone.

## Why we watch it

Roz begins by following her programming and ends up learning from the world around her, which makes her a good way into how machines learn and adapt. It is also a story about community, belonging and taking care of others. Students can compare how Roz learns with how apps learn what they like.

## What happens

Rodney Copperbottom is a young inventor from Rivet Town who grew up watching his dad wash dishes and dreaming of working for his hero, the famous inventor Bigweld. He travels to Robot City with an invention to show Bigweld, only to find that Bigweld Industries has been taken over by Ratchet. Ratchet has stopped making spare parts so older robots have to buy expensive upgrades, and he calls the robots who can't pay "outmodes." Rodney starts fixing broken robots for free, and finds out what it takes to stand up to a company that wants everyone to buy something new.

## Why we watch it

Robots is about robots who build, repair and invent, so it connects to engineering and to the idea that a good idea starts with noticing a need. It is also a good movie about perseverance, about how ads make us feel like we are not good enough, and about what to do with the money you have.

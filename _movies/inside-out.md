## What happens

Riley is eleven when her family moves from Minnesota to San Francisco. Inside her head, five emotions (Joy, Sadness, Anger, Fear and Disgust) run headquarters. When Joy and Sadness get lost far from headquarters, Riley is left with only Anger, Fear and Disgust while she struggles with a new school and a new home. Joy has to cross Riley's mind to get back, with help from Riley's old imaginary friend, Bing Bong.

## Why we watch it

This is the gentle first film in the Inside Out pair, and it fits students who are changing schools, friends or homes. It shows why sadness is useful and why pretending to be happy does not work. The worksheet is all reflection, with no computer science. One scene near the end is sad (Bing Bong), which is a good moment for the class to talk about.

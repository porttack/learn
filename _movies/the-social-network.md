## What happens

In 2003, Harvard sophomore Mark Zuckerberg is dumped by his girlfriend, writes an insulting blog post, and builds a site that lets students rate each other's photos. That gets him in trouble, and it also gets him an idea. He builds \"thefacebook\" with his friend Eduardo Saverin, and it spreads from school to school. Along the way he meets Sean Parker, the co-founder of Napster, and the friends start to fall out. The story is told through two lawsuits, one from Eduardo and one from three classmates who say Mark stole their idea.

## Why we watch it

This is a good-looking, fast-talking movie about the early days of a company students use every day. It raises questions about privacy, ownership of ideas, fairness between friends, and what a founder owes the people who helped. It is a dramatization based on a book, and the real people involved have said parts are made up, which makes it a good media-literacy lesson. It is rated PG-13, and Common Sense Media lists it for ages 14 and up: strong profanity, heavy drinking, drug use and sexual content. It is a good fit for older students. Preview it first.

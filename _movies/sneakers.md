## What happens

In 1969 two college friends hack a bank to send money to causes they support, and only one of them gets away. Years later, Martin Bishop runs a small San Francisco team that is paid to break into companies to show them their weak spots. Men who say they are from the NSA offer to clear his record if he steals a black box that can break the codes protecting computer networks. The job turns out not to be what it seems, and the team has to get the box back before it is used.

## Why we watch it

Sneakers is the most fun way to see what security testing is: a team gets paid to break in so the owners can fix the holes. It shows how many attacks work on people, not on computers, and it asks who should be allowed to have a tool that can break almost any code. It is rated PG-13, and Common Sense Media lists it for ages 13 and up, mostly for one strong word and a scene where a main character is held captive. It is a thriller with almost no blood.

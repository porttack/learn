## What happens

Coraline Jones moves to a rundown apartment house with parents who are too busy to play with her. She finds a tiny door that leads to an Other World where everything looks like her life, but better, and her Other Mother wants her to stay forever. To do that, Coraline would have to let her sew buttons over her eyes. Coraline has to outsmart the Other Mother to save herself and the children trapped there.

## Why we watch it

Coraline is brave, curious and stubborn, and the film asks what we do when we feel ignored and someone offers us exactly what we think we want. It is the scariest film on this list: it is rated PG for creepy images and scary scenes (button eyes, a monster that changes shape, ghost children). Check that your class is comfortable with that before you choose it. The worksheet is all reflection, with no computer science.

## What happens

After a huge flood sweeps through the forest where he lives, a solitary gray cat escapes onto a small sailboat. He ends up sharing it with a capybara, a ring-tailed lemur, a yellow Labrador and a secretarybird. They do not speak the same language, and nobody in the film speaks at all. The boat sails past giant stone pillars and a sunken city while the animals learn to work together and to be brave.

## Why we watch it

Flow won the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature in 2025. It is a short, quiet film from Latvia, and it has no dialogue at all, so students have to watch closely to understand the story. It shows trust, fear, teamwork and learning to live with change. It is rated PG for peril and thematic elements, and Common Sense Media lists it for ages 6 and up. Some parents report that the peril upsets sensitive children. The worksheet is all reflection, with no computer science.

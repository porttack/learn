## What happens

Carl Fredricksen is an old man who has lost his wife, Ellie, and is about to lose their home. He ties thousands of balloons to the house and floats away to Paradise Falls, the place they always wanted to see together. Russell, an eight-year-old Wilderness Explorer, is stuck on the porch. They meet a giant bird, a talking dog named Dug, and a famous explorer who is not what he seems.

## Why we watch it

Up is about grief, moving on and finding people to care about when you thought your adventure was over. The opening ten minutes are a sad, wordless story of a marriage, and some students will find it hard, so it is worth knowing ahead of time. The worksheet is all reflection, with no computer science.

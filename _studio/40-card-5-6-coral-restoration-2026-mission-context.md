## Core Question
Competition tasks simulate real coral restoration work: surveying reefs, placing fragments, monitoring recovery. In the real ocean, why do humans replant coral by hand, and does it work?

## Resource (before seminar, ~10 min)
Read the provided excerpt on coral bleaching and restoration methods (fragment nurseries, outplanting, substrate work). Note what causes bleaching and one honest limitation of restoration.

## Prep Notes (your artifact)
1. One claim: is restoration a solution, a stopgap, or a distraction? Reasoning required.
2. One specific way an ROV or diver-replacement robot changes the economics or safety of this work.
3. One honest question.

## Clearing This Card
Prep notes plus participation, or the written alternative.

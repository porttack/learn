## Students will be able to

- Use row and column counts to work out where ships must (and can't) be.
- Explain the difference between a sure step and a heuristic (a rule of thumb).

## Before class

- Print one [worksheet]({{ '/unplugged/solo-battleship/' | relative_url }}) per student.
- Print keep-going sheets from the [New set page]({{ '/unplugged/solo-battleship-generator/' | relative_url }}):
  Copies = class size, Sets per student = 2. Use **5x5 easy** for most
  students and **6x6** for the ones who fly through.

## The lesson

### 1. Hook (5 minutes)

Ask who has played Battleship. *"What if you got the answers to every
guess at once, as numbers on the edges? Could you find the fleet without
guessing?"*

### 2. The small one, together (10 minutes)

Do **Try a small one first** on the board as a class. Say every move out
loud: "This column says 0, so it's all water." Students copy along.

### 3. The puzzles (20 minutes)

Students solve the puzzles on their own. When one stalls, ask: *"Which row
or column is already full?"* Filling it with water is always a safe move.

### 4. Keep going (as students finish)

Keep-going sheets, smaller grids first.

### 5. Wrap-up (5 minutes)

Read the **Computer science connection** box together. Ask for one sure
step and one heuristic students used.

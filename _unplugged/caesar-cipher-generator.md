A fresh batch of Caesar cipher messages every time you click **New set**,
in any of three styles: decode with the key given, decode with no key (try
every shift), or a longer passage for frequency analysis. The set number
prints on the sheet. To reprint
the same set later, type its number back into the **Set #** box.

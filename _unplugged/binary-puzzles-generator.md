A fresh set of binary logic puzzles every time you click **New set**. Pick
a level first: 6 by 6 easy puzzles are solvable with just the tricks from
the [main worksheet]({{ '/unplugged/binary-puzzles/' | relative_url }}), no
guessing needed. The other levels are more reduced, so you'll lean on the
no-matching-rows-or-columns rule more often. The set number prints on the
sheet. To reprint the same set
later, type its number back into the **Set #** box.

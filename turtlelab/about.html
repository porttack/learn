TurtleLab

About TurtleLab

TurtleLab is a block-based drawing tool. You snap blocks together to tell a turtle where to walk, and it draws as it goes. It runs entirely in your web browser. There is nothing to install, no account to make, and nothing you do is sent anywhere.

It was made by Eric Brown for classroom use, as part of learn.porttack.com.

Read the guide or read the privacy page.

Where the look comes from

TurtleLab's blocks and its turtle are based on Turtle Blocks JS, part of the family of free learning software from Sugar Labs, written by Walter Bender and many other contributors. We adapted its block shape drawing code and some of its artwork (the turtle, the palette icons and some of the toolbar icons), and changed them to fit TurtleLab.

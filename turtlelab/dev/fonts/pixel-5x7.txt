; The letters of TurtleLab's `pixel` font, drawn by hand for TurtleLab (Eric Brown, 2026, AGPL-3.0-or-later like the rest of the app).
; They are not copied from any font file: each letter is a small picture made of square pixels, in the old 5 wide by 7 tall style of
; video games and dot matrix displays. The generator (dev/make-lettering-data.py, `read_pixels`) turns each into the outline of its pixels.
;
; Each letter starts with "@ " and the letter it is. # is a lit pixel and . is an empty one. The rows are listed from the top. A line of two
; dashes (--) marks the baseline: the rows before it sit on the baseline (the last of them just above it, so a letter with fewer rows is
; shorter, like a small letter), and the rows after it hang below it, like the tail of a g. Without a -- line, all the rows are above the baseline.
; Capitals and digits are 7 rows. Small letters with no tall part are 5 rows, and with one (b d f h k l t) 7 or 6. A letter is as wide as its
; widest row (an i is 1 wide, an m 5), and one empty pixel is left between letters. A mark that floats (a quote, a dash) lists its empty rows too.

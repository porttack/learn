**HOW THE ROBOT WORKS** These are the same rules as the AP CSP exam reference sheet. - The robot is the triangle. It points the way it's facing. - `MOVE_FORWARD ()` moves it one square the way it's facing. - `ROTATE_LEFT ()` and `ROTATE_RIGHT ()` turn it 90 degrees without moving it. - `CAN_MOVE (direction)` is true if the square next to the robot in that direction is open. The direction is `forward`, `backward`, `left`, or `right`, from the **robot's** point of view, not yours. - `GoalReached ()` is true when the robot is on the gray square. - Black squares are blocked. **If the robot tries to move into a black square or off the grid, it stays where it is and the program stops.** Tip: turn the paper so the robot points up before you work out a left or right turn.

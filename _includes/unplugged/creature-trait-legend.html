**THE FOUR TRAITS** Every creature on the grid is one of these two, for all four traits. There's no in-between: a creature is never "sort of" striped. - **round** body, or **square** body - **has_hat** (wearing one), or no hat - **two_eyes**, or one eye - **striped** body, or plain body The bold words (`round`, `has_hat`, `two_eyes`, `striped`) are what an expression uses to ask about that trait. Each one means "yes, this creature has it." For example, `round` is true for the round creatures and false for the square ones.

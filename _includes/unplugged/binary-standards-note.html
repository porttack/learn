HEADS UP: BINARY IS LEAVING THE NEWEST STANDARDS

CSTA's 2026 standards no longer include binary or data encoding in their core middle or high school standards; the new core makes room for AI, data science, and ethics instead. So this lesson aligns with CSTA 2017 and the California CS standards (built on 2017), not CSTA 2026.

AP CSP still tests it. Binary numbers are part of Big Idea 2 (Data), roughly 17 to 22 percent of the AP exam. What changed, and why.

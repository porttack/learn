**HOW TO PLAY** - Sit where you can't see your partner's page. - On your turn, call a shot by naming a row label, then a column label: for example, "row 101, column 011." To catch mistakes before they cost you a turn, say the decimal value too: "row 101, that's 5, column 011, that's 3." Your partner checks that the decimal matches before answering. - Your partner looks at that square on their own "My ships" grid and answers "hit" or "miss." - Mark the result on your own "My shots" grid: an **X** for a hit, a dot for a miss, at that row and column. That grid is how you remember where you've already fired. - Take turns calling shots. The first player to hit every square of the other player's fleet wins.

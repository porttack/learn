## The two-number contest

<p class="fill-line-paragraph">
An object sinks, floats, or hovers depending on two numbers: its own
<span class="fill-line short"></span> pulling down, and the weight of the
water it <span class="fill-line short"></span> pushing up. Exactly equal,
the object is at <span class="fill-line short"></span> buoyancy.
</p>

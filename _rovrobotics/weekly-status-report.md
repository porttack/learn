Every Thursday, in the last five minutes of studio, you post a status report. This is the real version of the thing every engineer eventually does at a real job: a short update on what happened, what is next, and what is in the way.

## The format

```
Hours this week: 3.5

Progress
- what exists now that did not exist last Thursday

Near-term goals
- what you will show, and when

Concerns
- what is in your way, and who or what unblocks it
```

Three bullets in each section, max. One line per bullet. Under ten lines total. If it takes you more than five minutes to write, you are overwriting it.

## Where it goes

One Schoology discussion thread per week, whole class, one post each. Post from your phone if that is faster. If there is something you do not want to post where the whole class can see it, tell the teacher directly, at the checks corner or by message. That counts the same as posting it.

## Your hours

The line at the top is your timecard. That record belongs to you. The rule that keeps it honest: an hour has to be attached to something in Progress. One hour with nothing to show for it happens to everyone sometimes. Three weeks of it in a row is a conversation, not a number.

## What "not yet" looks like

```
Progress: I worked on the claw a lot this week and made good progress.
Near-term goals: Keep working on the claw and finish it.
Concerns: None.
```

Three specific problems. "A lot" is not a quantity, so there is nothing here anyone can check. "Finish it" is not a state anyone can point at on Thursday and say yes or no. And "none," three weeks running, means either a miracle happened or you stopped looking for what is actually in your way.

## What clears

```
Hours this week: 3.5

Progress
- Jaw prototype v2 printed. Closes on 1.5 in PVC at the bench, slips on the round sample.
- Measured servo stall current at 1.4 A, under our 2 A fuse.

Near-term goals
- Reprint the jaw with a 2 mm deeper tooth profile, test Tuesday.
- Write the servo vs stepper DDR before sprint review.

Concerns
- The 2 mm change may not clear the frame rail. Need ten minutes with the frame team Thursday.
- Waiting on M3 standoffs ordered Sep 22. If they miss next week the mount slips a sprint.
```

Every Progress bullet names a thing that now exists, plus a number or an observation. Every goal has a date and a state someone can check. Every concern names a person to talk to or a date at risk. Notice that every bullet here is a fair question for a 90 second oral check. That is the actual test of whether a bullet was worth writing.

## How this gets checked

Filed or not filed, that is the only thing tracked week to week. Nobody is scoring your bullets line by line.

At the checks corner, expect the teacher to pick one Progress bullet from your own report and ask you one live question about it. Same rule as everything else on this site: written work is preparation, the live question is the check.

At sprint retro, your team compares its sprint commitment against what its members actually reported in Progress those three weeks. That comparison is the real audit, and your team runs it, not the teacher.

The Concerns column across the whole class thread becomes Tuesday's agenda. If something is blocking you, this is the fastest way to get it unblocked, because other people read this thread too, and some of your concerns get solved by a teammate before the teacher ever sees them.

## About AI

Use whatever tools you use to write this. Your teacher uses AI for status reports sometimes too. The bar is not where the words came from, it is whether the bullets are true, because you will be asked about one of them out loud.

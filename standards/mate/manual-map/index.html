Where the Manual Builds Each Skill

A cross-reference compiled here from MATE's competition manuals for Scout, Navigator and Ranger. It is not a standard and not tied to one year. About this catalog.

The manual is laid out in five parts. This page shows what each part asks a team to do and which skills and competencies that builds, plus two items that are not parts: props and leadership. It is a pointer for finding things, not a standard. A locator, not a standard. Each row is this project's reading of what a manual section asks students to do; the skills shown are those of the competencies listed. Props and Leadership are added here: Props is a subsection of Part 1, and Leadership is not a section at all. Which items count, and the points, differ by class and year; check the current manual.

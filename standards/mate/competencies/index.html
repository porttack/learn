What MATE Builds: the Competencies

A cross-reference compiled here from MATE's competition manuals for Scout, Navigator and Ranger. It is not a standard and not tied to one year. About this catalog.

What MATE builds, as an outline: three areas, and under each the things a team has to be able to do. Open an item for what it means, where MATE shows it, the MATE skills it develops, and the standards it draws on, each with how fully it fits and why. 1 of 27 are linked to a lesson here so far, and 58 standards links are listed. A strength says how well the standard matches the item, not how well any lesson teaches it. For a shorter view see the standards competency map or the standards skill map.

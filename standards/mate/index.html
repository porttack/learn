Where the employer skills come from

The two employer lists are from the World Economic Forum's Future of Jobs Report 2025, a global survey of more than a thousand employers. It asks which skills they call most essential today and which are growing fastest toward 2030. Because it is a survey of what employers say, it shows what the workplace asks for, not what any course teaches; that is why this site sets it beside MATE's skills, the standards and the lessons.

One chart, eight views. The first two are the World Economic Forum's lists of the ten skills employers called most essential in 2025 and the ten growing fastest to 2030.

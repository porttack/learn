MATE ROV Competition: What It Builds

A cross-reference compiled here from MATE's competition manuals for Scout, Navigator and Ranger. It is not a standard and not tied to one year. About this catalog.
Start: skills and competenciesWhat MATE says it buildsCompetencies, as an outlineStandards mapSkill by skillSkills and Ocean 10 chartThe Ocean DecadeThe classesManual to skills

MATE says its competition builds eighteen skills: nine for the workplace and nine specific to ROVs, and each skill has parts (the smaller bubbles beside it, from Evaluate-Compete). Click any bubble for what it means, what builds it, the employer skills it matches, and the standards it draws on. The competencies themselves are on their own page, as an outline.

Orange bubbles are the employability skills and green the ROV-specific ones, the same split MATE uses for its icons. A darker bubble means a lesson here covers it, directly or through a competency that builds it.

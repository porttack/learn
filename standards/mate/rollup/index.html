MATE ROV Skill by Skill

A cross-reference compiled here from MATE's competition manuals for Scout, Navigator and Ranger. It is not a standard and not tied to one year. About this catalog.
Start: skills and competenciesWhat MATE says it buildsCompetencies in fullStandards mapSkill by skillSkills and Ocean 10 chartThe Ocean DecadeThe classes

Eighteen sections, one per MATE skill, to scroll through. Each lists the employer skills from the World Economic Forum's 2025 and 2030 lists that match it, the UN Ocean Decade challenges reached through its competencies, the competencies that develop it, and the standards from every framework those competencies draw on, with a line on each.

MATE tags each mission task with Ocean Decade challenges, not each skill, so a skill's challenges here are reached through its competencies. MATE also tags the whole competition with challenges 9 and 10, as it labels them.

Employability skills (Evaluate-Compete general outcomes): Communication · Creativity · Entrepreneurship · Autonomy · Ability to Deal with Obstacles · Intellectual Development · Critical Thinking and Problem Solving · Project Knowledge and Skills · Teamwork/Collaboration
ROV-specific skills (MATE ROV Evaluate-Compete outcomes): Vehicle Design, Buoyancy, Propulsion · Systems Design · Structure · Control/Electrical · Sensors/Payload/Tools · Safety · Project Management · Technical Presentation · Technology and Society

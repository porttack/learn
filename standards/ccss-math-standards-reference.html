Middle school only (grades 6-8), and only the strands Bootstrap:Algebra's own alignment claims touch: 6.NS (The Number System), 6.SP/7.SP/8.SP (Statistics & Probability), 7.EE (Expressions & Equations), 8.F (Functions), 8.G (Geometry, Pythagorean theorem cluster only).

Common Core ELA (Speaking, Listening & Writing) Standards Reference

What this is. A locally built index of Common Core State Standards for English Language Arts & Literacy (National Governors Association Center for Best Practices, Council of Chief State School Officers) (2010). Scope. Speaking & Listening and Writing strands only, grades 6 through 11-12 (five bands: 6, 7, 8, 9-10, 11-12) -- not Reading (Literature or Informational Text) or Language, and not K-5. California's own CCSS adoption (the 2013 ELA/Literacy framework) keeps this same numbering verbatim, so this catalog serves both a national and a California-specific citation. Codes. Codes are CCSS's own (e.g. SL.6.1, W.9-10.1), not a project-specific scheme. The lettered sub-parts some standards carry officially (e.g. W.6.1.a-e) are folded into one paraphrase per numbered standard here, matching this project's existing topic-level (not sub-EK-level) citation granularity elsewhere (see apcsp.json's own topic/LO split). What this is not. (c) 2010 National Governors Association Center for Best Practices and Council of Chief State School Officers, all rights reserved; NGA/CCSSO license the standards for free noncommercial educational use, but every paraphrase here is written for this project, never the standards' own text, matching this repo's policy for every other catalog regardless of a source's own licence.
